Privacy

Your data

What we keep

When you register, apply, order or write to us, we keep your name, email, phone (if you give it) and your answers to the form.

Why

To send your ticket or reply, check you in at the door, and contact you about that event or application. We never sell it or share it with sponsors.

Who sees it

Only the TEDxGUC board members who run that event or recruitment round.

How long

Event registrations for the season they belong to. Job applications are deleted six months after the round closes, unless you join the team.

Deleting it

Write to us from the contact page and we’ll delete your data within 30 days.
